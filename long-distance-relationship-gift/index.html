What happens when they open it

  1. A night map lights up both your cities, with the real distance between them.
  2. They fly your paper plane along the dotted path. The kilometres count down as it goes.
  3. Stop 1: a rainy window. They wipe the fog to find your photos, one by one.
  4. Stop 2: the phone rings. They pick up, and everything you miss about them arrives as message bubbles.
  5. Stop 3: a parcel. They tear the tape and your letter writes itself in.
  6. At 0 km the plane unfolds into a boarding pass with both cities. They pick the day you meet, and the date comes to your email.
  7. It ends with the two of you together on the map.

What to write when you miss someone

  • Small things land hardest: "your voice notes at 1 am", "stealing fries off your plate".
  • Caption each photo with where you were and what you'll do there next time.
  • In the letter, talk about the next visit, not only the distance.
  • Ask a question they can answer with a date: "When do we meet?" or "Which weekend can I steal you?"
  • Works across countries too: type any city, or the distance yourself for a small town.

Questions people ask

Something personal that arrives instantly. A Miss You surprise website carries your photos, the things you miss and your letter across a map from your city to theirs, so it feels like it travelled to them, and it ends by asking when you'll meet.

Does it work if we live in different countries?

Yes. Type both cities and we work out the distance. For a small town, you can type the kilometres yourself.

Can I see it before paying?

Yes. The editor plays the whole surprise with your own photos and words while you write. You only pay when you're happy with it.

Do they need to install an app?

No. It's a private web link that opens in any browser on a phone or laptop. Send it on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere else.

Who can see it?

Only people who have the link. Customer surprises are hidden from Google and never listed anywhere.

How long does it stay live?

At least 12 months from the day you order, so they can open it again whenever they like.

How do I pay?

UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay. Your link appears on screen straight away and we email it to you too.
