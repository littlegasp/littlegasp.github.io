What happens when they open it

  1. They ring the doorbell and a note slides out from under the door.
  2. They unroll a treasure map with four clues marked on it. The map stays one tap away for the whole hunt.
  3. The house is dark, so they search each room with a torch. Warm and cold hints help, and a soft glow appears if they get stuck.
  4. Behind the photo frame: all your reasons. In the cookie jar: a sweet note. Inside a book: your letter. Under the cushion: a film reel.
  5. They follow a trail of rose petals to the balcony door.
  6. An old projector plays your photos as a little film, all by itself.
  7. They tap the ring box, it opens, and your question appears. Their yes is emailed to you.

Ideas for a proposal letter

  • Start with the first moment you knew, in one plain sentence.
  • Name small, real things you love: "how you hum while making chai".
  • Use photo captions as tiny chapters of your story, in order.
  • Keep the question short and in your own words: "Will you marry me?" or "Will you be mine, always?"
  • Plan where you'll be when they open it. Many people send the link and wait in the next room with the real ring.
