Questions

Good to know

Can I see it before paying?

Yes. You see a full live preview with your own photos and words before you pay anything.

How fast will I get my website?

Instantly. Your link appears on screen as soon as payment is done, and we email it to you too.

Does the person need to install anything?

No. It's a normal web link that opens in any browser on phone or laptop.

How long does the website stay live?

At least 12 months from the date of your order.

Who can see it?

Only people who have the link. It isn't listed anywhere or shown in search results.

Can I change something after I get the link?

Yes. Email us with your payment ID within 7 days and we'll fix typos or swap photos for free.
