About Little Gasp

Little Gasp (littlegasp.com) is an online gift studio from Mumbai, India. We make personalised, interactive surprise websites: little painted worlds that the other person taps, swipes and plays through, with your photos, your notes and your letter inside.

A card is read once. A Little Gasp surprise is something they play, and usually play again. You write it in a few minutes, watch the whole thing with your own photos before paying, then send one private link on WhatsApp. No app to install.

What we make

  • Sealed with Love – a love letter they open: a dove delivers it, they crack the wax seal, unfold your notes and watch a quill write every word.
  • Memory Lane – an anniversary ride: helmets on, kick-start the scooter and drive up a road where every milestone is one of your photos, ending with a love lock on a bridge.
  • Birthday Cake – candles, a wish and confetti, then your photos and notes, ending with a birthday selfie with a crown.
  • Make a Wish – a teddy bear's birthday party: light the candles, pop balloons, unwrap gifts and cut the cake.
  • Mend the Heart – a proper sorry: they mend a broken heart with gold and tell you honestly how mad they still are.
  • Karwa Chauth Moon – raise the channi to the moon, light the diyas and pass the puja thali for seven promises.
  • Diwali Night – trace a glowing rangoli and write wishes in the sky, for a partner, family or Bhai Dooj.
  • Garba Circle – light all nine nights of Navratri, then a dandiya round to the beat.

Every design can be tried free. Make yours here.

How it works

Pick a design, add their name, your photos, a few little notes and a letter. The preview plays the whole surprise as you write. When you're happy, pay ₹99 or ₹149 by UPI or card, and your private link appears straight away (we email it too). It stays live for at least 12 months and only people with the link can see it.
