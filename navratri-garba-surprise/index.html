What happens when they open it

  1. The circle starts. Each tap on the dancing couple lights the next of nine night colours.
  2. Every night opens a photo, a reason or that night's Devi card.
  3. With all nine lit, a dandiya round begins: tap when the ring closes on the sticks.
  4. Your letter, your question, and a happy ending.

Reasons for nine nights

  • "You never miss a step, even when I do."
  • "Your laugh in the middle of the circle."
  • "Every year, my favourite partner."
