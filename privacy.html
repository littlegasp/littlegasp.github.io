Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what Little Gasp collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Order details: your email address, payment ID and the design you chose.
  • Website content: the names, photos, notes, letter, messages and any song you upload for your website.
  • Payments: handled entirely by Razorpay. We receive confirmation that you paid, never your card or bank details.

How we use it

Only to build and deliver your website, send you your link, and help you if you contact us. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't use your photos or messages for advertising without your written permission.

Who can see your website

Your website is private by link: anyone who has the link can open it, so share it only with people you choose. Websites aren't listed on our site or submitted to search engines.

Where it's stored

While you're creating your website, your draft (including photos and any song) stays only on your own device. After payment, your content is stored in our Google account (Google Sheets and Drive) so your website can be shown. The website pages are hosted on GitHub Pages.

How long we keep it

We keep your content while your website is live, normally 12 months. You can ask us to delete your website and all your content at any time.

Cookies

Our website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, email littlegasp.orders@gmail.com. We'll act on your request within 7 days.
