What happens when they open it

  1. They tap a helmet onto each of you, then swipe the kick lever to start the scooter.
  2. A paper map unfolds with the years you've been together.
  3. They hold the fuel nozzle, and each bar on the gauge is one reason you love them.
  4. They drive up a winding road. Every photo is a milestone with its year and caption.
  5. At the toll gate, each lift of the barrier shows a promise.
  6. You stop for chai, clink glasses, and your letter is written on the chalkboard.
  7. On the bridge they lock a love lock and throw away the key, then ride into forever.

Milestone ideas for your photos

  • The first photo you ever took together.
  • The first trip, even if it was only to Lonavala.
  • The day you moved in, or the wedding day.
  • A silly everyday photo. Those are often the favourites.
  • This year: "Still my favourite person to sit in traffic with."

Questions people ask

Memory Lane takes up to 20 photos, each with a short caption like "2019: our first trip".

Yes. The editor plays the whole surprise with your own photos and words while you write. You only pay when you're happy with it.

No. It's a private web link that opens in any browser on a phone or laptop. Send it on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere else.

Who can see it?

Only people who have the link. Customer surprises are hidden from Google and never listed anywhere.

How long does it stay live?

At least 12 months from the day you order, so they can open it again whenever they like.

How do I pay?
