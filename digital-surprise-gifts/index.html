What makes it different from an e-card

  • They do something on every screen: light candles, crack a wax seal, mend a broken heart, drive a scooter up memory lane.
  • Your photos, little notes and letter appear as rewards along the way, so they take their time with every word.
  • Many designs end with a question you write ("Dinner on Saturday?") and send their answer straight to your email.
  • It works on any phone or laptop browser. Nothing to install, nothing to print, nothing to ship.

How to make one in about five minutes

  1. Open the editor and pick a design.
  2. Add their name, up to five photos with captions, a few short reasons and your letter.
  3. Watch the live preview play exactly as they will see it.
  4. Pay by UPI or card. Your private link appears instantly and is emailed to you.
  5. Send the link on WhatsApp or Instagram, or save it for midnight.

Questions people ask

What is a digital surprise?

A digital surprise is a gift you send as a link instead of a parcel. On Little Gasp it's a small interactive website made just for one person, with your photos and words, that they tap through like a tiny game.

Is this a good last-minute gift?

Yes. You can make it in a few minutes and the link is ready the moment you pay, so it works even on the day itself, or when you're in a different city.

Can I see it before paying?

Yes. The editor plays the whole surprise with your own photos and words while you write. You only pay when you're happy with it.

Do they need to install an app?

No. It's a private web link that opens in any browser on a phone or laptop. Send it on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere else.

Who can see it?

Only people who have the link. Customer surprises are hidden from Google and never listed anywhere.

How long does it stay live?

At least 12 months from the day you order, so they can open it again whenever they like.

How do I pay?

UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay. Your link appears on screen straight away and we email it to you too.
