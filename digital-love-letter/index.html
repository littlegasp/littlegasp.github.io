What happens when they open it

  1. A dove postman flies in with an envelope. They tap it.
  2. They press and hold the wax heart until it cracks, and the envelope really opens.
  3. They drag the folded letter open, and each crease shows one reason you love them.
  4. A quill writes your letter live, word by word.
  5. Pressed flowers turn into polaroids of your photos. Dated memories wait in a keepsake box.
  6. They join stars into a heart and your initials appear. A hidden P.S. shows only when they warm the paper with a candle.
  7. They untie a ribbon to find your question, and can write back with a thumbprint seal. Their reply comes to your email.

Love letter ideas

  • Start with the moment you knew, in one or two plain sentences.
  • Write reasons as small true details: "the way you hum while cooking".
  • Put one memory per photo caption, with the date if you remember it.
  • Save one line for the hidden P.S. Something just for them.
  • Keep the question simple and kind: "Will you be mine, again and always?"
