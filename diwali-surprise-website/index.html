What happens when they open it

  1. They light the diya, then trace a dotted circle to colour a glowing rangoli.
  2. They tap the sky to launch fireworks. Each burst writes one of your wishes in light.
  3. They release lanterns, and each one leaves a polaroid of your photos.
  4. They draw with a sparkler and save the picture.
  5. They read your letter. If you hid a real gift, they scratch the gold foil on a mithai box to find it.
  6. They send a lantern back with their own wish, which comes to your email.
