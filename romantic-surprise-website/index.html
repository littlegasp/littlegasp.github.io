What happens when they open it

  1. A dim room, rain on the window, and a record leaning on the shelf with a note: "Play me."
  2. Your first photo is the album cover, with their name as the title.
  3. They slide the record out, lift the arm and drop the needle. The music starts, softly crackling.
  4. Side A: every reason you love them is one track. The needle moves inward with each one.
  5. They flip to Side B, and your photos open one by one with your captions.
  6. Inside the sleeve, your letter types itself out as the liner notes.
  7. At the end, a hidden track: your question. They can say yes, or "Play it once more first". Their answer comes to your email.
  8. It ends with two people slow dancing in lamplight.

Questions people ask

Is it only for girlfriends?

No. The record, the room and the words work for anyone you love: girlfriend, boyfriend, wife or husband.

Can I use our own song?

Yes. Upload your MP3 or M4A and it plays from the moment they drop the needle. Otherwise a soft late-night piano tune plays.

Can I see it before paying?

Yes. The editor plays the whole surprise with your own photos and words while you write. You only pay when you're happy with it.

Do they need to install an app?

No. It's a private web link that opens in any browser on a phone or laptop. Send it on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere else.

Who can see it?

Only people who have the link. Customer surprises are hidden from Google and never listed anywhere.

How long does it stay live?

At least 12 months from the day you order, so they can open it again whenever they like.

How do I pay?

UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay. Your link appears on screen straight away and we email it to you too.
