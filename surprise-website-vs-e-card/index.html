When an e-card is enough

For a colleague's birthday or a quick festival wish to many people, an e-card is fine. It's fast, and nobody expects more.

When a video message is better

If you are comfortable on camera, nothing beats your own face and voice. A video works especially well for grandparents and family who would rather see you than read.

When a physical gift wins

If you're in the same city and they've been wanting something specific, give them the thing. A surprise website doesn't replace a hug or a present they can hold. It works well alongside one, though: send the link the night before and hand over the gift the next day.

When a surprise website is the right pick

  • You're in a different city or country, or the day has already started.
  • You have more to say than fits in a card, but writing a long text feels flat.
  • You want them to take their time with every line, not scroll past it.
  • You're asking something (sorry, a date, a question) and want an honest answer back.

Questions people ask

Is a surprise website better than an e-card?

It depends on the moment. An e-card is quicker and fine for light occasions. A surprise website takes about five minutes, holds your photos, notes and a full letter, and they play through it, so it suits birthdays, anniversaries, apologies and long distance.

Can I send a surprise website with a physical gift?

Yes. Many people send the link at midnight and give the gift in person later. The website carries the words; the gift carries the hug.
